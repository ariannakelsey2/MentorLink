# MentorLink
Students seeking academic or career guidance struggle to find appropriate faculty or peer mentors who match their interests and goals. Faculty willing to mentor lack efficient ways to connect with students, leading to missed mentorship opportunities and underutilized expertise.

# Features
- Profile matching based on research interests, career goals, and backgrounds
- Availability scheduling and meeting coordination
- Mentorship goal setting and progress tracking
- Anonymous feedback and relationship quality assessment
- Success story repository and outcome tracking
- Group mentorship and cohort formation tools
